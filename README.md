From the Industry Talk 2, I gained insights into the role of technology, innovation, and system development in supporting business operations. The speaker shared how project management and structured system development processes are essential in delivering effective technological solutions. During the talk, he emphasized that system development is the process of creating a software application rather than code writing. He mentioned that system development is similar to house building, where a clear blueprint is essential. In software, the blueprint is the System Development Life Cycle (SDLC). In addition, he also identified the importance of project management in system development by describing how project management effectively controls project scope, development timeline and budget. This talk helped me understand how technology teams plan, develop, and manage systems to ensure efficiency, innovation, and alignment with business goals.

Furthermore, the speaker also highlight the foundational skills that are needed to achieve success in computer science, which are programming proficiency in different programming languages, problem-solving skills and critical thinking, deep understanding of data structures and algorithms and communication skills and teamwork to collaborate with others. Overall, the talk increased my awareness of the importance of strong leadership and innovation in the technology field. It encouraged me to develop better problem-solving and project planning skills.
